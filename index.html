Rubik’s Cube — 54-sticker demo

Face turns are permutations of 54 facelets. Scramble, then apply the inverse — you return to the identity (solved). Based on the local derivation demo; TheMathFlow video is inspiration only (no on-screen formulas invented here). Not a CFOP solver.

如何运行 demo

      

        本页的 Apply scramble / Apply inverse 与仓库里的
        python/demo.py 是同一条路：拧乱再取逆，回到已解（单位元）。
        默认拧法 R U R' U'。
      

      
在仓库根目录执行（Python 3.10+，仅标准库）：

      
python3 python/demo.py
python3 python/demo.py "F R U R' U' F'"
